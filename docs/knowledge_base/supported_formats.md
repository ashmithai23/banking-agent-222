# Supported Document Formats for Knowledge Ingestion

VectraBank Knowledge Ingestion supports:

1. **PDF Documents (`.pdf`)**:
   - Institutional annual reports
   - Commercial lending policy handbooks
   - Account fee disclosures and schedule sheets
2. **Markdown Documents (`.md`)**:
   - Structured internal policies
   - Developer documentation and runbooks
   - Agent system prompt templates
3. **Plain Text (`.txt`)**:
   - Raw regulatory bulletins and press releases
   - Disclaimers and legal terms
