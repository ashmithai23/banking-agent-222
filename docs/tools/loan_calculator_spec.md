# Loan Calculator Specification & Formulas

## Debt-to-Income (DTI)
$$DTI = \frac{\text{Total Monthly Debt Obligations}}{\text{Gross Monthly Income}} \times 100$$
- **Qualifying Standard**: Maximum 43% DTI for prime conventional mortgages.
- **FHA Standard**: Maximum 50% with compensating cash reserves.

## Monthly Amortization Formula
$$M = P \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$$
Where:
- $M$ = Monthly mortgage payment
- $P$ = Principal loan amount
- $r$ = Monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12)
- $n$ = Number of payments (Years * 12)
