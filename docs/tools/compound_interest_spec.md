# Compound Interest & Wealth Growth Model

## Formula
$$A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} + PMT \times \frac{\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} - 1}{\frac{r}{n}}$$

Where:
- $A$ = Final account balance
- $P$ = Initial principal deposit
- $r$ = Annual interest rate (decimal)
- $n$ = Compounding frequency per year ($n=12$ for monthly, $n=365$ for daily)
- $t$ = Total investment horizon in years
- $PMT$ = Regular recurring contribution made each compounding period
