# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Rules Specification

## Regulatory Background
Enforces provisions of the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and USA PATRIOT Act.

## Rule Definitions
1. **BSA-001 (Cash Threshold)**: Transactions strictly $\ge \$10,000.00$ require mandatory CTR filing.
2. **BSA-002 (Structuring Heuristic)**: Multiple deposits between $\$8,000$ and $\$9,999$ within 14 calendar days flag suspicious activity report (SAR) investigation.
3. **BSA-003 (High-Risk Cross-Border)**: Wire transfers routed through jurisdictions on the FATF grey or black list require secondary AML compliance officer sign-off.
